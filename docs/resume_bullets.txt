HFT FPGA PROJECT — RESUME BULLET POINTS
========================================

FULL VERSION (2 bullets)
------------------------
- Architected and verified a fully-pipelined FPGA tick-to-trade engine in SystemVerilog (NASDAQ ITCH parser, 256-order limit order book, depth-weighted imbalance strategy, pre-trade risk checks), achieving 205 ns deterministic tick-to-trade latency (41 cycles @ 200 MHz, zero jitter) - ~5x faster than an optimized C baseline while eliminating the CPU's ~1 ms tail-latency spikes.

- Drove the design through full Vivado synthesis and place-and-route on a Xilinx UltraScale+ FPGA (timing-closed at 200 MHz, ~7K LUTs, 200 MB/s line-rate ITCH ingest) and validated correctness with 81/81 cocotb tests against a bit-accurate Python golden model.
