What am I looking at?

Speed / FPGA vs SW
The FPGA computes a trade decision in a fixed 205 ns. The software path here takes microseconds and varies (jitter). "FPGA vs SW" is the median ratio; "time saved" is cumulative SW compute minus what the FPGA would have taken. The project's real edge is this latency + determinism, not the signal.
Taker vs Maker
Taker crosses the spread (pays it) — usually ~break-even after fees. Maker posts two-sided quotes to earn the spread, skewing on inventory. Switch between them with the control button.
Pre-trade funnel
Raw signals must pass a gauntlet — fair value (microprice), edge vs cost, volatility/liquidity/staleness, inventory skew, exposure. Each veto is counted; only survivors become orders. (Maker mode bypasses the gauntlet.)
P&L / drawdown / kill switch
Realized = booked; unrealized = open, marked to mid. Per-symbol max DD is the biggest peak-to-current drop. The kill switch halts all trading on a daily-loss/drawdown breach; reset it from Controls.
Exposure
Gross = long+short notional (capital at work), net = long−short (directional risk), capped by the gross limit.
Controls & config
Start/stop/pause, switch strategy, tune threshold / maker half-spread / fee / caps live, add or remove a symbol, and export the trade log to CSV. Data source badge shows SIM (synthetic) vs live.
Keyboard shortcuts
Space start/pause · m switch maker/taker · t theme · 1–4 tabs. (Ignored while typing in a field.)
Trade stats
Profit factor = gross wins ÷ gross losses (>1 is profitable). Sharpe* is a rough per-fill mean/σ of the equity curve — a consistency proxy, not annualized.
